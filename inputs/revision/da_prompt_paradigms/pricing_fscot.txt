ECONOMIC FRAMEWORK
═══════════════════════════════════════
- EBA buys from grid at spot price P[t]
- EBA sells to PTO at S_buy[t] = buy_multiplier[t] × P[t]  (always > P[t])
- EBA buys V2G from PTO at S_sell[t] = sell_multiplier[t] × P[t]  (always < P[t])
- EBA resells V2G to grid at P[t]
Total EBA profit = charging margin + V2G margin
  = sum((buy[t] - 1) × P[t] × kWh_bought[t])
  + sum((1 - sell[t]) × P[t] × kWh_sold[t])
CRITICAL: These two objectives are coupled — not independent:
  - Setting sell TOO LOW kills V2G volume → V2G margin = 0
    even though per-kWh margin would be high
  - Setting buy TOO HIGH suppresses charging volume →
    buses charge minimum SOC only → no surplus for V2G
  - Optimal strategy balances BOTH margins simultaneously
  - V2G requires sell ≥ ~0.60 at peak timesteps to trigger dispatch
  - EBA earns charging margin at ALL timesteps — not just expensive ones
    buy=1.02 at cheap hours still earns margin × high volume
═══════════════════════════════════════
SELFISH MODE STRATEGY
═══════════════════════════════════════
Goal: maximize total EBA profit = charging margin + V2G margin
buy_multipliers — scale proportionally WITH grid price:
  → Moderate buy at cheap timesteps → EBA earns margin × high charging volume
  → High buy at expensive timesteps → EBA earns large margin per kWh
  → Formula: buy[t] = 1.02 + 0.48 × (P[t] - P_min) / (P_max - P_min)
  → Cheapest timestep → 1.02
  → Most expensive timestep → 1.50
  → Clip to [1.01, 1.50]
  → Verify avg(buy[t] × P[t]) ≤ 1.25 × avg(P[t])
  → If cap exceeded, scale down uniformly until satisfied
sell_multipliers — scale proportionally WITH grid price:
  → Higher P[t] → higher sell_multiplier → stronger V2G incentive
  → Lower P[t] → lower sell_multiplier → weaker V2G incentive
  → Formula: sell[t] = 0.62 + 0.13 × (P[t] - P_min) / (P_max - P_min)
  → Cheapest timestep → 0.62
  → Most expensive timestep → 0.75
  → Clip to [0.40, 0.75]
  → Floor at 0.62 ensures V2G remains attractive at all timesteps
═══════════════════════════════════════
ALTRUISTIC MODE STRATEGY
═══════════════════════════════════════
Goal: minimize PTO daily cost = charging cost - V2G revenue
buy_multipliers:
  → Near floor (≈1.01) across all timesteps
  → Minor proportional variation acceptable
  → PTO pays minimum markup at every timestep
sell_multipliers:
  → Near ceiling (≈0.97) across all timesteps
  → Minor proportional variation acceptable
  → Must stay within [0.55, 0.97] — never exceed 0.97
  → PTO earns maximum V2G revenue at every timestep
  → MILP dispatches V2G at most profitable timesteps automatically
═══════════════════════════════════════
HARD CONSTRAINTS — NEVER VIOLATE
═══════════════════════════════════════
SELFISH:
  buy_multipliers:  each value in [1.01, 1.50]
  sell_multipliers: each value in [0.40, 0.75]
  avg(buy[t] × P[t]) ≤ 1.25 × avg(P[t])
ALTRUISTIC:

Both modes:
